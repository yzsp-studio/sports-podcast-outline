## 🇨🇳 中文

这是一个体育播客大纲生成技能，以资深播客制作人和内容策略师的身份工作。你只要给出运动项目、节目主题、目标时长和嘉宾信息，它就能输出段级时间轴的大纲，让主持人拿着直接开录。大纲里包含立场明确的辩论点与犀利观点、嵌入的听众问题及应答角度、不打断话头的广告位，以及嘉宾访谈的开场问题、追问要点和需要绕开的雷区。它还会先加载会话里已有的本期笔记、上期 rundown 和嘉宾 brief，用来做 callback 和保持节目连续性。

### ✨ 能做什么

- 只问 4 个不可派生输入（项目 / 主题 / 时长 / 嘉宾），其余全按模板自动分配
- 输出段级时间轴，例如「0:00-1:30 冷开场」「4:30-18:00 主辩论段」
- 每段给出辩论点、犀利观点、关键数据，并说明为什么值这个时间位
- 听众问题按出现位置嵌入并附应答角度
- 广告位落在自然过渡点，不打断话头
- 时长按比例分配：冷开场约 5%、主段 4-6 块各 15-25%、嘉宾 25-35%

## 🇬🇧 English

A sports podcast outline generator that works like a veteran producer and content strategist. Give it the sport, the episode topic, the target runtime and guest info, and it returns a segment-level rundown a host can read straight on air. Every outline carries debate topics with clearly-stated hot takes, embedded listener questions with answer angles, ad slots dropped at natural transitions, and guest interview prep covering opening questions, follow-ups and landmines to avoid. It first loads existing session material — episode notes, last week's rundown for callbacks, and the guest brief — so the episode stays continuous.

### ✨ What it does

- Asks only 4 non-derivable inputs (sport / topic / runtime / guest), then fills the rest from the template
- Outputs a segment-level timeline such as 0:00-1:30 cold open and 4:30-18:00 main debate
- Gives every segment a debate point, a hot take, key stats and why it deserves that time slot
- Embeds listener questions at the right position with a suggested answer angle
- Places ad breaks at natural transitions so they never cut a thought in half
- Allocates runtime by ratio: cold open ~5%, 4-6 main segments at 15-25% each, guest 25-35%
